# Source-linked monthly preparation and independent review

Run migration `024_monthly_settlement_review.sql` after migrations 018–023 in the existing Preview project. Suggested SQL Editor label: **20 月度结算来源关联与独立复核**. This stage creates immutable internal preparation/review records, not published client statements or wallet charges.

## Workflow

1. Administrator reads a closed natural month for the actual advertiser and explicitly hands the source to its correct client company.
2. In pricing, administrator saves an appropriate quote version for the same company and month with the monthly source spend and verified fee terms. An internal quote alone does not prove customer acceptance. Keep actual fee-agreement evidence outside the platform; record its reference.
3. Administrator or scoped finance preparer opens `/zh/operations/settlements`, selects the company, complete source and matching-month quote, and confirms the agreement reference and preparation note.
4. Another administrator or scoped finance reviewer reviews the immutable preparation. The preparer cannot review their own record, including an administrator. Approval/rejection is final for this draft; exact retries are idempotent. Correct a rejected preparation by saving a new quote revision and preparing another draft, preserving provenance.
5. Approved means **reviewed, not published/debited**. Statement publication and atomic ledger posting are the separate explicit administrator action introduced in migration 025 below.

## Checks and limits

Database checks enforce active advertiser-company binding, explicit handoff, CAD source currency, same company and natural month, a month closed in the advertiser timezone, validated calendar-month coverage, verified scope, and exact spend agreement. Migration 026 extends coverage to reconciled aggregate zeros or independently reviewed inactivity, without rewriting raw missing rows (see MONTHLY_ACTIVITY_COVERAGE.md). Unverified missing rows are never treated as zero. The full month's micro-unit spend is rounded once half-up to CAD cents and must equal the quote's original advertising cost. Archived quotes cannot be prepared or approved. Binding pause blocks new preparation/review and finance projection until restored.

Quote selection is one account/month source per quote version. A quote cannot be attached to another source afterward. Alternative quote versions remain separate internal drafts, never additive charges. Preparation cannot change the quote calculation or original source data. Fees/FX remain in the administrator-only quote snapshot; finance sees only CAD source cost, CNY draft total, references, notes, version IDs and status through a scoped RPC. Neither raw API responses nor private FX/evidence are projected. The new tables have no direct authenticated grants; all mutations and finance projections use capability-checked security-definer functions with MFA.

The preparation link copies the existing quote's calculated result; it does not turn an agreement reference into an enforceable contract or certify an arbitrary quote as billable. No customer publication, scheduled collection, refund, intercompany remittance, tax calculation or money movement is added. Posting in 025 recomputes the quote in the API, rechecks authorization and approval, serializes account/month posting, enforces sufficient funds, and atomically links the client statement to the exact immutable debit lines. Multiple reviewed drafts must never create duplicate client charges.

## Formal client publication and ledger posting (025)

Run `025_monthly_statement_posting.sql` after 024. SQL Editor name: **21 月度账单正式发布与余额记账**. This enables a new explicit administrator action on independently approved drafts. Preparation/review still do not post money. The administrator must verify agreed charges and supply a client-visible Chinese summary before selecting **正式发布并扣余额**.

The API recalculates the immutable quote using its locked historical rate and rules and compares all monetary outputs before calling the posting RPC; it never trusts browser amounts or refreshes a reference rate at publication. SQL rechecks independent approval, active ownership/handoff, quote lifecycle, source and copied amounts. A CNY wallet and sufficient posted funds are required. Existing account-month publications, including legacy published reports, block a second charge. The unique binding/month constraint is shared across all quote/source versions for that account.

The posting transaction locks the wallet, creates one immutable client statement, records the upstream advertising/execution amount as `ad_spend` and China operating plus agreed customer-borne transfer charges as `service_fee`, and appends an audit event. Zero lines produce no zero-valued ledger entry. A deferred constraint trigger verifies exact matching ledger lines at transaction commit. Any failure rolls back the entire statement/debit operation. Exact draft+summary retries return the original receipt even after later access pause; changed summaries conflict. Account-month alternatives cannot charge again. Wallet serialization is compatible with existing bank-receipt posting. This does not pay a bank, remit Canada funds or modify an ad platform budget.

Clients open `/zh/account/statements` from the account center to view published CNY charges and daily impressions/clicks. Their existing posted-wallet balance and transaction list reflect the same ledger entries. The client RPC explicitly requires organization membership plus finance access; internal staff access alone does not grant client statement access. The projection excludes raw source responses, CAD source cost, private FX, procurement margins and agreement evidence. Published statements remain accessible under client finance permissions even if internal source handoff is later paused; revoking client finance access blocks future reads.

Scope: one advertiser account/month per statement, latest 100 client records and 50 internal receipts. There is no automatic scheduling, tax/invoice issuance, auto-bank remittance, multi-account combined invoice, cancellation or correction/refund workflow. A formal statement cannot be edited or deleted. Corrections require a future controlled adjustment/credit workflow, never republishing another version to charge the month again. The source PDF requirement of legacy monthly_ad_reports is not bypassed: API-derived statements use their own immutable record and provenance through reviewed draft/source/quote IDs. No migration seeds or posts real financial data.

Migration 026 adds the activity-aware coverage paths described in [MONTHLY_ACTIVITY_COVERAGE.md](MONTHLY_ACTIVITY_COVERAGE.md), including zero-use and not-active months. It keeps the separate independent financial review and atomic posting protections in this document.
